About the Experience

Where every meal is a fresh start

Irreconcilable Bites is the world's first restaurant dedicated to the beautifully messy process of moving on. Whether you're celebrating a finalized decree, nursing a broken heart, or just really into dark comedy and good food, we have a table for you — and yes, it's a table for one.

Our award-winning chef crafts each dish as a metaphor for relationships: complex, occasionally bitter, and best when you stop overthinking it. The ambiance is candlelit, the music is melancholy jazz, and the staff has been trained to never ask "Will anyone be joining you?"

Tonight's House Rules

Please respect the establishment

  1. No couples holding hands. This is a healing space.
  2. Crying at the table is permitted but limited to 15 minutes per course.
  3. If your ex walks in, the staff will seat them in the other hemisphere of the restaurant.
  4. All phones must be face-down. We don't need to see who's not texting you.
  5. Tipping is mandatory. Your server is also going through something.
