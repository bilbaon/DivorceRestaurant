Events & Promotions

Something to look forward to. Finally.

January — New Year, New You

  • Resolution Dissolution Dinner

    Burn last year's relationship goals for a free drink.

  • Clean Slate Seating

    New tablemates every course. Because starting over is the theme.

February — Anti-Valentine Month

  • Ex Photo Shredding Night

    Bring an ex photo → shred it → free dessert.

  • Solo Diner Discount

    Dine alone → 20% off. Self-love tastes like savings.

  • Couples Surcharge

    Couples are welcome, but there's a 15% happiness tax.

March — Finalization Season

  • Champagne Toast

    Show proof of finalized divorce → complimentary champagne toast.

  • Paperwork Party Nights

    Notary onsite. Sign your papers over apps and cocktails.

April — Tax Trauma Month

  • Write-Off Wednesdays

    Itemize your emotional damages. Our staff will nod sympathetically.

  • Surprise Fee Fridays

    Surprise fees randomly added to bills. Just like real life.

May — Rebound Season

  • Rebound Date Special

    Bring a rebound date → first drink free. No questions asked.

  • "Just a Friend" Applause

    Staff applauds if you introduce someone as "just a friend."

June — Wedding Season Survival

  • Open Bar Support Group Night

    For everyone who's been to three weddings this month. Open bar. Open hearts.

  • Registry Regret Raffle

    Bring an unused wedding registry item. Win prizes. Release the attachment.

July — Independence Month

  • Freedom Feast Fireworks Dinner

    Celebrate personal independence with a multi-course feast and indoor sparklers.

  • Confetti Cannon Moments

    Confetti cannons fire if someone says "I'm better off."

  • Breakup Anthem DJ Night

    DJ plays breakup anthems only. No love songs. No exceptions.

August — Hot Mess Summer

  • Messy Margarita Nights

    Oversized margaritas. Oversized emotions. Undersized napkins.

  • Ex Text Draft Contest

    Best unsent message wins a prize. Worst one gets framed on the wall.

September — Back to Separate Lives

  • Co-Parenting Trivia Night

    Test your knowledge. Categories include "Who Gets the Dog?" and "Schedule Conflicts."

  • Kids Eat Free Night

    Kids eat free if they can name both parents' lawyers.

October — Haunted Relationships

  • Dress as Your Ex's Worst Trait

    Costume contest. First place wins therapy voucher. Second place needs one.

  • Ghosting Speed Dating Night

    Meet people! Then disappear without explanation. It's tradition.

November — Thanks-For-Nothing Month

  • Awkward Family Seating Night

    We seat you with strangers who judge your life choices. Just like the holidays.

  • Passive Aggressive Potluck

    Bring a dish. Leave a note. "I made this better than you ever could."

December — Holiday Survival Season

  • Silent Night Dinner

    No talking allowed. Just eating and meaningful stares. Peace at last.

  • Ugly Sweater Breakup Brunch

    Wear the sweater they hated. Eat pancakes shaped like broken hearts.

  • Midnight Ball Drop

    The ball drop is replaced with signed settlement papers falling from the ceiling.
